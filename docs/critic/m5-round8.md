# Milestone 5 (polish and perf), critic round 8

Screenshots: 27 frames (win run 01 to 20 with the asteroid split from the standalone showcase
run, lose run 21 to 24, pause menu, how to fly and settings 25 to 27).

## Critic findings (summary of the verbatim report)

Every pitch feature appeared in at least one frame except a convincing asteroid split in this
set (the showcase frame caught the pieces overlapping); the lock tone, roll and AI behaviours
cannot be judged from stills.

1. Minor: the Leviathan never reads as a capital ship: a slab of white light strips under an
   orange hex "pancake", first seen close and edge-on; in the cutscene plain boxes with two
   smokestack-like towers, coming apart around a white flash.
2. Minor: the generator fight is the busiest scene (hex grid, deck lights, bloom, rocks, tags);
   the pale player ship blends into the lit deck.
3. Minor: the HUD blocks and the radio box cover about a fifth of the frame and ships slip
   behind them; a long carrier radio line stacks with a new one.
4. Minor: the Mauls still read as small twin-pod "dumbbells"; the bracket double bar is not
   explained in the guide.
5. Cosmetic: the asteroid split frame reads as a hit flash.

Runners-up (cosmetic): HULL CRITICAL shares the SHIELDS DOWN orange, the guide repeats its
cards in a table, the second wingman's name label comes and goes, the wave-clear radio line
repeats word for word.

WORST: minor - the Leviathan never reads as a capital ship.

## Builder response (final round, round 9)

This is the fourth round in a row with no major problem; the worst item has been the carrier's
art in each, described differently every time (a barge, a plank, oil derricks, smokestacks).
The last round of changes is small and concrete:

1. The carrier arrives about 1.3 km out and three quarters on (not broadside at 1 km), so the
   first look after the warp streak shows its length and deck; its window strips glow at half
   strength so the hull, not a slab of white strips, is what reads; the finale core is fire
   coloured.
3. The carrier briefing radio line lasts 6 s instead of 8 s.
Also: HULL CRITICAL is red (SHIELDS DOWN stays orange) and the wave-clear call varies by wave.
