# Milestone 1 (greybox), critic round 4 (final for M1)

Screenshots: 19 frames copied to a scratchpad folder outside the repo; the critic ran from there, so
only the screenshots and the pitch reached it (no project files).

## Critic findings (summary of the verbatim report)

1. Major: most required effects are missing (bloom on lasers and explosions, engine trails, nebula,
   shield shimmer); explosions are hard-edged polygons; boosting only changes numbers.
2. Major: the player's ship sits in the middle of the view and hides targets just below the nose.
3. Major: the carrier finale is an anticlimax (dark slab carrier, one flat polygon explosion, no
   wreckage); the objective said "Clear the blast zone" while the camera showed only the carrier.
4. Major: enemies are specks at 440 to 780 m; no shot shows a bomber shape or a strafing run.
5. Minor: the aim point still gets crowded (friend ring, lock box, labels) in busy moments.

WORST: major - Most of the brief's required effects (bloom, engine trails, nebula, shield shimmer)
are missing, so lasers and explosions are flat hard-edged shapes.

## Builder response and M1 exit

- The worst remaining problem is the effects pass, which the milestone plan assigns to M2 (nebula,
  models) and M3 (trails, bloom, shimmer, explosions). Per the DECISIONS entry on greybox critic
  scope this counts as cosmetic for the greybox, so the M1 loop exits here.
- Fixed before closing M1: chase camera raised and pulled back (ship sits in the lower third, clear of
  the crosshair); finale objective text reworded; the playtest now also captures a close-range Maul.
- Carried into M2/M3: carrier model and break-up, enemy models with strong emissive lights, engine
  trails, bloom, explosion particles, nebula backdrop, remaining reticle crowding.
