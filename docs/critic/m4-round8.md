## Critic findings (summary of the verbatim report)

The critic found nearly every pitch item on screen, one clean look across title, HUD, pause,
victory and failure screens, and no blockers.

1. Major: one bomber approach takes the player from healthy to HULL CRITICAL in seconds (the AI
   lead fix made the Maul's heavy plasma accurate), and hits on the player's own shield barely
   show; the first loud warning is HULL CRITICAL.
2. Major: the target is a small white spark under the markers (hit sparks cover the ship being
   shot); some untargeted enemies read as unmarked.
3. Minor: HUD pieces still pile up at times (bomber pairs, a generator diamond on the nose, a
   wingman ring over the objective panel, a stale radio line, a "shields restored" banner while
   the bar reads 90).
4. Minor: the carrier finale is target practice against a plank.
5. Minor: the asteroid split does not read; fights happen in open nebula.

WORST: major - a single bomber approach takes you from healthy to HULL CRITICAL while hits on your
own shield barely show.
